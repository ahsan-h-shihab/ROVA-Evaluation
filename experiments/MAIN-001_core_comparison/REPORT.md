# MAIN-001 — Core Comparison (frozen manifest rev. 5)

**Status:** Complete · **Executed:** 2026-07-08 · **Code:** commit
`7f624ca27a86fbbbe67afc68ebb886a4edbb67a2` (verified at launch and archived
in `config.json`).

## Objective

Execute the frozen rev. 5 minimum campaign supporting the primary claim:
*"the runtime-governance (ROVA) versus baseline verdict changes across the
operating-cost spectrum, and survives the containment check, under matched,
fairly tuned, nested-CV-validated evaluation."* Execution-only — no
redesign, no hyperparameter/environment/cost-grid/protocol change.

## Design (as frozen)

- **45 nested-CV-HPO units** = 3 methods (`static`, `ewma`, `rova`) × 3
  environments × 5 cost ratios.
- **Environments:** `favorable_hmm_coupled`,
  `unfavorable_oscillation_uncoupled`, `collapse_moderate_coupled`.
- **Cost-ratio grid:** `[0.5, 1.5, 3.5, 8.0, 20.0]`.
- **Budget:** K_out=5, K_in=3, n_trials=80, seeds=60 (0..59). Full env scale
  verified in-checkpoint (horizon=250, n_agents=10, density=0.3, coupling=0.3).
- **Free byproducts:** 15 containment (DR4) reports; CVaR-95 co-primary;
  BCa CIs; Wilcoxon+Cliff's δ+Holm (primary, per cost-ratio); Friedman+Holm
  (secondary omnibus).
- **DP-oracle validation** (`unfavorable_oscillation_uncoupled`): appendix-level.

## Execution

15 shards (`N = min(15, cores)`), one work-unit each, launched detached via
`setsid` with BLAS/OMP threads pinned to 1 per process
(`OMP/OPENBLAS/MKL/NUMEXPR_NUM_THREADS=1`) to prevent oversubscription of the
scipy-openblas backend. No CPU affinity (15 single-threaded procs spread
naturally across 16 physical cores). Wall-clock ≈ **1 hour**; zero `[FAIL]`.

## Deviation (infrastructure only — no scientific-data impact)

**Manifest lost-update race.** `run_pipeline._mark_done` + `utils.save_json`
do non-atomic, unlocked read-modify-write on one shared `manifest.json`; at 15
concurrent shards this loses updates (live manifest ended with 4/61 entries).
Latent bug — the parallel path was validated only at 2 shards, where
collisions are rare.

**Why the data is safe:** each per-unit HPO checkpoint, containment report,
and the DP-oracle JSON is written by a *single* shard (units never overlap
across shards). All were validated: **45/45 checkpoints at exactly 5 folds
with finite regret + CVaR**, **15/15 containment reports**, **DP certified**.

**Remediation (user-approved: "continue + reconstruct"):** manifest
reconstructed deterministically from the authoritative artifacts via
`raw/rebuild_and_validate.py`, which validates the 61 required entries while
assembling → **61/61, 0 problems**. The raw raced file is preserved at
`raw/manifest.live_raced.json`. No code/hyperparameter/environment/cost-grid/
protocol change.

## Results (see `stats_summary.json`; regenerated by `analysis.py`)

Unit of analysis = scenario-instance seed; pooled per-seed regret (n=60),
per-seed aligned across methods (identical outer-fold split, seed0=0).

**Primary claim supported: the ROVA-vs-baseline verdict changes across the
cost spectrum in all three environments** (`verdict_changes_across_spectrum =
True` each). Direction is consistent: at low cost-ratios ROVA does not beat
the baselines; at high cost-ratios ROVA wins decisively (Holm-significant,
Cliff's δ → −1.0, order-of-magnitude lower regret and CVaR).

| Environment | c0.5 | c1.5 | c3.5 | c8.0 | c20.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| favorable_hmm_coupled | ROVA_loses | ROVA_loses | ROVA_wins | ROVA_wins | ROVA_wins |
| unfavorable_oscillation_uncoupled | mixed | ROVA_loses | mixed | ROVA_wins | ROVA_wins |
| collapse_moderate_coupled | ROVA_loses | mixed | mixed | ROVA_wins | ROVA_wins |

("wins" = ROVA lower median regret than *both* baselines with Holm-significant
Wilcoxon; "loses" = higher than both; else "mixed". This is a descriptive
verdict label over the frozen statistics — final claim adjudication via the
decision rules DR1–DR8 is a downstream writeup task.)

**Containment (DR4):** `prop_scale`/`stab_scale` engaged (non-collapsed) in
13/15 cells; 2 collapse-to-zero cases reported, not hidden.

**DP-oracle:** `certified_equivalent = True`, max discrepancy 4.51e-14
(floating-point noise) — myopic oracle == sequential oracle.

**Distribution-shift (optional/bonus, revision-stage):**
`raw/distribution_shift_report__{rova,ewma}.json` produced by the finalize
step. NOT a completion criterion (runbook §10).
